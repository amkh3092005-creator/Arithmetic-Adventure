About the Educational Program

Comprehensive Program Overview & Instructional Plan

📘 Program Overview

Arithmetic Adventure is an interactive educational mathematics program designed to help Grade 4 primary-school students understand and practise arithmetic operations through simple explanations, worked examples, guided practice, interactive questions, and immediate feedback.

Target Learners

Grade 4 primary-school students studying mathematics in English.

Instructional Language

English (Title, Instructions, Lessons, Quizzes, and Feedback).

🎯 Specific Learning Objectives

  • Solve addition problems involving multi-digit numbers.
  • Subtract multi-digit numbers, including problems requiring regrouping.
  • Understand multiplication as equal groups and place-value strategies.
  • Solve simple division problems and relate division to multiplication.
  • Apply the correct order of operations in mixed-operation problems.
  • Solve simple real-life mathematical word problems.
  • Check answers and learn from immediate corrective feedback.
